## <span style="font-family: Arial;">**Buying power**</span> 

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Buying power is the amount of money you can use to purchase stocks, options, or crypto.</span>

## <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**1\. Overnight Buying Power**</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Overnight buying</span> p<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">ower</span> = <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">the lesser of (2×</span> m<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">argin</span> e<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">xcess) and the greater of (2× SMA, 2× Reg-T</span> e<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">xcess)</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Overnight buying</span> p<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">ower</span> <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">is the amount available to purchase securities intended to be held overnight.</span> <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">After liquidating an overnight position,</span> o<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">vernight buying</span> p<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">ower</span> <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">is replenished by the sale proceeds, adjusted based on the maintenance requirement of the position sold.</span>

## <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**2\. Day Trad**</span>**e** <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**Buying Power**</span>

Day trade buying power = <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">4× your</span> m<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">argin</span> e<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">xcess</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Profits from day trades are added to your cash balance immediately but do not increase</span> day trade buying power <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">intraday. These profits will be reflected in</span> day trade buying power <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">on the next trading day.</span> <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">If you begin the day by liquidating positions held overnight,</span> day trade buying power <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">is replenished based on the lower of the previous day</span><span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">’</span><span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">s closing price or the sale price</span>.

## **3\. Option** <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**Buying Power**</span>

Option <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">buying power = your</span> available cash

Option buying power is only available after you enable options trading.

## <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**4\. Crypto Buying Power**</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Crypto</span> <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">buying power is calculated based on your available cash, pending settlements, and the margin or collateral currently reserved for other positions.</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Crypto</span> buying power is only available after you enable <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">crypto</span> trading.

## <span style="font-family: Arial;">**Margin call**</span>

## <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">**1\. Required**</span> **Maintenance Call**

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Y</span>ou may receive a required maintenance call <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">when m</span>argin equity falls below maintenance requirement. <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">When you trigger an</span> required maintenance <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">call, you can only close positions</span>. P<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">lease deposit funds or reduce positions to restore margin excess.</span>

## **2\. Reg-T Call**

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Y</span>ou may receive a Reg-T call when Reg-T excess ≤ 0 and SMA ≤ 0. <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Liquidating to meet a Reg T call may result in a violation. To avoid a violation, please add funds by the deadline.</span>

Ongoing violations within a 12-month period will result in the following:

-   3rd violation: Overnight BP restriction
-   4th violation: 90-day close only restriction

## **3\. Equity Maintenance Call**

If you flagged as PDT, you may receive an equity maintenance call when margin equity falls below $25,000. <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">When you trigger an</span> equity maintenance call, a<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">ny new day trade will trigger a 90-day close only restriction.</span>

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">What is PDT？</span>

The full name of PDT is Pattern Day Trader. Executing 4 day trades within 5 business days will mark your account as a Pattern Day Trader. <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Once marked as a PDT, you are eligible for a one-time reset to remove this mark. Please contact customer service to apply for this waiver.</span>

## **4\. Day-Trade Call**

<span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">Y</span>ou may receive a <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">d</span>ay <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">t</span>rade call <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">when d</span>ay <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">t</span>rade <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">buying power</span> exhausted. You should deposit funds by the deadline to cover the deficiency.

Ongoing violations within a <span style="font-family: 微软雅黑;">12</span>\-month period will result in the following:

-   1st violation: Day trade buying power is limited to your MME for 90 days. During this period, selling positions will not release additional buying power.
-   2nd violation: Account restricted to close only for 90 days.

**5\. Concentration Margin Call** 

You may receive a concentration margin call when your account holds an overly concentrated position in one or more stocks. You should deposit funds or reduce your concentrated position before the deadline.

**Trigger conditions** — all three must be met:

-   Enable margin investing
-   Cash debit balance exceeds $100,000
-   CM Excess < 0 (Concentration Requirement > Margin Equity)

**How CM Excess is calculated:**

CM Excess = Margin Equity − Total Concentration Requirement

The Concentration Requirement for each stock is based on its CM Ratio (single stock market value ÷ total marginable holdings market value):
